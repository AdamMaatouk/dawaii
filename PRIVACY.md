# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Dawaii is a medication reminder app. It is designed so that your information never leaves your phone.

## What Dawaii stores

Everything you enter stays on your device:

- your medications, schedules, photos of pills and pill counts
- the doses you take, skip or postpone
- blood pressure and blood sugar readings
- your settings, such as language, text size and your first name if you choose to enter it

## What Dawaii collects

Nothing. Dawaii has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The Android app does not request internet access.

## Permissions

- **Notifications and alarms:** to remind you when a dose is due.
- **Camera and photos (optional):** only if you add a photo of a medication. The photo is kept inside the app.

## Sharing

Information leaves your phone only when you choose to send it:

- **Backups:** you save a backup file and decide where it goes. Backups do not include photos.
- **Doctor report:** a PDF you can print or share with the app of your choice.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a medication removes its history. Uninstalling Dawaii deletes everything it stored on your phone.

## Children

Dawaii is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Medical disclaimer

Dawaii is a reminder tool. It does not give medical advice, and the ranges it shows for blood pressure and blood sugar are general guides, not a diagnosis. Always follow your doctor's instructions.
